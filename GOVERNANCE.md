# Governance

ThreadMesh is currently maintained by the Fuyo AIC organization. Governance will evolve as independent contributors and adapter maintainers join.

## Decision model

- Editorial and non-normative changes may be merged through ordinary review.
- Normative protocol changes require a public design issue and maintainer approval.
- Security-default changes require an explicit threat analysis.
- Durable, cross-cutting, or difficult-to-reverse decisions require an ADR.
- Breaking changes are allowed before `1.0`, but must be documented and versioned once releases begin.

## Maintainer responsibilities

- Protect context sovereignty and least-authority defaults.
- Separate documented behavior from aspiration.
- Avoid vendor-specific assumptions in the core protocol.
- Require evidence before claiming adapter conformance.
- Disclose relevant affiliations and conflicts of interest.
- Provide a clear rationale when rejecting substantial proposals.

## Adapter ownership

Each production adapter should eventually have named maintainers, a supported harness version range, conformance results, and a documented capability matrix. An adapter may progress independently of the core specification but cannot redefine core intent semantics.

## Becoming a maintainer

Maintainer invitations are based on sustained, high-quality contributions, sound judgment around safety and compatibility, and constructive participation. There is no contribution-count threshold.
