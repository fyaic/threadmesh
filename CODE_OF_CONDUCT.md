# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience level, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people.
- Be specific, constructive, and willing to revise your position.
- Respect privacy and do not publish private task data, prompts, credentials, or logs.
- Disclose conflicts of interest when evaluating competing protocols or products.
- Assume good faith while taking safety concerns seriously.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or sustained personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Deliberately introducing hidden telemetry, unsafe defaults, or deceptive examples.
- Using project spaces to coordinate abuse or unauthorized access.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the contact method listed in the GitHub organization profile. Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants when necessary.

Enforcement decisions should be documented privately, applied consistently, and minimize unnecessary disclosure of reporters or affected people.
